01 / ABOUT SAI PAVAN

Built around the way facilities really work.

Pavan Naidu is the Founder & Director of Sai Pavan Integrated Facility Services, bringing a combination of Civil Engineering knowledge and MBA-level management expertise to facility management and maintenance solutions.

His approach connects technical site requirements with organized operations and client coordination, helping bring multiple facility needs under one coordinated service model.

  • Civil Engineering background with practical understanding of buildings, maintenance and site requirements.
  • MBA-qualified professional with a management-focused approach to operations and client coordination.
  • Focus on integrated facility management, bringing multiple maintenance requirements under one coordinated service approach.
  • Strong emphasis on quality, timely execution and professional coordination.
  • Understands both technical requirements and business/management needs of clients.
  • Provides a single point of contact for facility-related requirements.
  • Focused on building long-term client relationships and dependable maintenance support.
  • Leads the company with a vision to provide professional, organized and customized facility solutions.
